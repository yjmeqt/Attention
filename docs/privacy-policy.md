# Attention Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26 · [中文版](#zh)

Attention ("the app") respects your privacy.

**We don't collect personal data.** The app has no accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

**How links are processed.** When you save or share a link, the app fetches that link directly from your device, reads its public metadata (title, preview image, site icon) and renders card images on your device. For richer previews, some links are looked up through public endpoints:

- YouTube links: YouTube oEmbed (youtube.com)
- X (Twitter) links: FxTwitter public API (api.fxtwitter.com)
- Bilibili links: Bilibili public API (api.bilibili.com)
- Site icons: the site itself, and Google's favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons)

These requests contain only the link and are handled by those services under their own privacy policies. The developer never receives your links.

**On-device storage.** Links and images you save are kept in your library on your device, in storage shared by the app and its share extension. Text in saved images is recognized on the device so you can search it; it isn't uploaded. You can delete items in the app at any time; uninstalling the app removes them.

**Camera and photos.** The camera is used only to scan QR codes; frames are not saved or uploaded. Saving a card to Photos requires your permission. When you pick a photo to scan a QR code, the app can access only the photo you select.

**Children.** The app doesn't collect information from children.

**Changes.** Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

**Contact.** See the [support page](support.html).

## 中文 {#zh}

生效日期：2026-10-26

Attention（以下简称“本应用”）尊重并保护你的隐私。

**我们不收集任何个人数据。** 本应用没有账号系统，不包含广告或第三方统计 SDK，也不会追踪你在其他应用或网站上的行为。

**链接如何处理。** 当你保存或分享一个链接时，本应用会直接从你的设备访问该链接，读取公开的网页信息（标题、预览图、网站图标），并在设备上生成卡片图片。为了获得更完整的预览，以下链接会通过对应的公开接口获取信息：

- YouTube 链接：YouTube oEmbed 接口（youtube.com）
- X（推特）链接：FxTwitter 公开接口（api.fxtwitter.com）
- 哔哩哔哩链接：哔哩哔哩公开接口（api.bilibili.com）
- 网站图标：网站本身，以及 Google 网站图标服务（google.com/s2/favicons）

这些请求只包含链接本身，由对应服务按其隐私政策处理。开发者不会收到这些链接。

**本地存储。** 你保存的链接和图片会存放在设备上的收藏库中（应用与其分享扩展共享的存储空间）。图片中的文字在设备上识别，仅用于搜索，不会上传。你可以随时在应用内删除，卸载应用会同时删除这些数据。

**相机和相册。** 相机仅用于扫描二维码，画面不会被保存或上传。保存卡片到相册需要你的授权；从相册选择图片识别二维码时，本应用只能访问你选中的那一张。

**儿童隐私。** 本应用不面向儿童收集任何信息。

**变更。** 如本政策有更新，我们会在此页面公布。

**联系我们。** 如有任何问题，请通过[支持页面](support.html)联系我们。
