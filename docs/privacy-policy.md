# Attention Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10 · [简体中文](#zh) · [繁體中文](#zh-hant)

Attention ("the app") respects your privacy.

**We don't collect personal data.** The app has no accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites. The developer never receives your links, images or searches.

**How links are processed.** When you share a link into the app, it fetches that link directly from your device, reads its public metadata (title, preview image, site icon) and renders the card image on your device. For richer previews, some links are looked up through public endpoints:

- YouTube links: YouTube oEmbed (youtube.com)
- X (Twitter) links: FxTwitter public API (api.fxtwitter.com)
- Bilibili links: Bilibili public API (api.bilibili.com)
- Site icons: the site itself, and Google's favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons)

These requests contain only the link and are handled by those services under their own privacy policies.

**Storage and iCloud sync.** Links and images you save are kept in your library on your device, in storage shared by the app and its share extension. If you are signed in to iCloud, the library also syncs through your own iCloud private database, so the same items appear on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. That data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it. You can delete items in the app at any time, which removes them from iCloud as well. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on that device.

**Text in images.** Text in saved images is recognized on the device so you can search it; it isn't uploaded.

**Visual search model (optional).** Searching images by description uses the MobileCLIP model, which isn't included in the app. If you choose to download it in Settings, the app fetches a model file (about 100 MB) from the developer's GitHub Releases (github.com). If github.com cannot be reached, it tries the public mirrors ghfast.top and mirror.ghproxy.com, which relay the same file and see only the download request, not your library. The file is verified against a known checksum before use. The model runs entirely on your device, and you can remove it in Settings.

**Apple Intelligence.** On devices where Apple Intelligence is enabled, asking the library a question in natural language and generating search keywords use Apple's on-device language model. Your questions and library contents are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer.

**Camera and photos.** The camera is used only to scan QR codes; frames are not saved or uploaded. Saving a card to Photos requires your permission. When you pick a photo to scan a QR code, the app can access only the photo you select.

**Children.** The app doesn't collect information from children.

**Changes.** Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

**Contact.** See the [support page](support.html).

- X（推特）链接：FxTwitter 公开接口（api.fxtwitter.com）

**Apple 智能。** 在已开启 Apple 智能的设备上，用自然语言向收藏库提问、以及生成搜索关键词，会使用 Apple 的设备端语言模型。你的问题和收藏内容都在设备上处理，不会发送给开发者。

**相机和相册。** 相机仅用于扫描二维码，画面不会被保存或上传。保存卡片到相册需要你的授权；从相册选择图片识别二维码时，本应用只能访问你选中的那一张。

**儿童隐私。** 本应用不面向儿童收集任何信息。

**变更。** 如本政策有更新，我们会在此页面公布。

**联系我们。** 如有任何问题，请通过[支持页面](support.html)联系我们。
