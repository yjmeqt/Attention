Paste a YouTube or X link in WeChat and your friend sees a bare URL: no title, no cover, no idea what it is. Attention turns any link into an image card with the title, cover, source and a QR code, so it reads at a glance in any chat, and the QR code opens the original.

CARDS FOR CHAT
Some chat apps, WeChat among them, don't preview links from YouTube, X and many other sites. Send the card instead. Sharing as an image also copies the link, so you can paste it right after the card.

ONE TAP FROM SHARE
In YouTube, X, Safari or any other app, tap Share and choose Attention. Or copy a link and paste it in the app. Previews bring in the page title, cover and site when available; sites that require a login or restrict access may have limited previews.

THE RIGHT TEMPLATE
Card, Text or QR templates size themselves to the content, and the one that fits is picked for you. Open the original link, share it or copy it at any time.

KEEP WHAT YOU SHARED
Every link you turn into a card stays in your library. Add tags, move items to Private, where viewing requires device authentication, or archive them.

FIND IT AGAIN
Search titles and text recognized in saved images, or filter by site or tag.

SCAN
Scan QR codes with the camera or find links in a selected photo. Made for iPhone and iPad, with dark mode support.

ON YOUR DEVICE
No account required. Cards are rendered and image text is recognized on your device, and your library syncs through your own iCloud. Loading previews contacts websites and, for some previews, third-party public services. See the privacy policy for details.
