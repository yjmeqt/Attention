Attention keeps the links and images you want to come back to.

SAVE AS YOU GO
Save links and images from the share sheet in Safari or other apps, or paste a link. Previews bring together page titles, covers and site details when available. Sites that require a login or restrict access may have limited previews.

ORGANIZE AND FIND
Add tags and filter by site or tag. Search titles and text recognized in saved images. Archive items for later, or move them to Private, where viewing requires device authentication.

SHARE WITH CONTEXT
Open the original link, share it or copy it. Turn links into image cards with a QR code, using Card, Text or QR templates that size themselves to the content. Sharing as an image also copies the link so you can paste it after sending the card.

SCAN AND SAVE
Scan QR codes with the camera or find links in a selected photo. Made for iPhone and iPad, with dark mode support.

KEPT ON YOUR DEVICE
No account required. Your library, image text recognition and card rendering stay on your device. Loading previews contacts websites and, for some previews, third-party public services. See the privacy policy for details.
