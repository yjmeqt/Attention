Links from YouTube, X and many other sites show up in WeChat as a bare URL, with no title and no thumbnail. Attention turns any link into a clean image card, so the people you share with can see what it is at a glance.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Copy a link, or tap Share in Safari or another app and choose Attention
2. Get a card with the title, cover image, author and a QR code
3. Send the image in WeChat, then paste the link — it's already on your clipboard

WORKS WITH
• YouTube videos: thumbnail, title and channel
• X posts: text, author and media
• Bilibili videos: cover, title and uploader
• Any web page: title, preview image and site icon

CARD STYLES
• Picks the best template for each link automatically: image, post, text or QR
• 4:5, 1:1 and 9:16 aspect ratios for chats, Moments and stories
• Scan the QR code on the card to open the original link
• Tracking parameters are removed from shared links

ALSO
• Scan a QR code with the camera or from a photo
• History, so you can reshare any card
• iPhone and iPad, light and dark mode

PRIVACY
Links are processed on your device. No account, no tracking, no data collected.
