Keep the links you want to return to in one place on your Mac. Paste a link into Attention to save its title and preview. Search your library, organize links with tags, and protect private items with Touch ID or your Mac login password.

From the menu bar, turn a link into an image card with a title, cover and QR code. Choose Card, Text or QR, then copy the image, save it as a PNG, or drag it into another app. Copy a link and press Control–Option–Command–A to put its card on the clipboard.

Your library syncs through iCloud across devices using the same Apple account. No Attention account is required.
